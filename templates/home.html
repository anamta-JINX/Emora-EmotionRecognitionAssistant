About

Making Emotions a Little
Less Mysterious

EMORA is a real-time emotion recognition app designed to help individuals with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) understand facial emotions during everyday interactions. Powered by computer vision and deep learning, EMORA provides instant, clear emotional insights.

Why EMORA?

  • Real-time emotion detection — no waiting, no guessing
  • ASD-focused design — simplicity first
  • Portable social support — fits in your pocket
  • Confidence booster — clear guidance, no pressure

Who is it for?

  • Individuals with ASD
  • Parents and caregivers
  • Educators and therapists
  • Anyone who believes empathy can be assisted by technology

EMORA isn't here to replace human connection — it's a quiet guide, providing clarity and support.

Under the Hood

How EMORA Works

EMORA integrates computer vision and deep learning to transform facial expressions into meaningful emotional insights — accurately, efficiently, and in real time.

Face Detection

Haar Cascade classifiers detect human faces from images and live video streams with high speed and low latency.

Emotion Classification

A Convolutional Neural Network analyzes facial features and maps them to discrete emotional states.

Multi-Dataset Training

Trained on three independent facial emotion datasets, improving robustness, diversity, and generalization.

Feedback Dataset

A dedicated feedback dataset captures user corrections, enabling iterative refinement and real-world adaptation.

Training & Evaluation

Structured training, validation, and testing pipelines with augmentation and regularization prevent overfitting.

Real-Time Inference

Optimized inference delivers instant predictions with confidence scoring for smooth live interaction.

EMORA's technology is designed to remain invisible — offering clarity without cognitive overload.
