Frequently Asked Questions

Does EMORA work in real time?

Yes. EMORA supports live webcam-based emotion analysis in addition to image upload analysis.

Can I upload an image instead of using a camera?

Yes. Users can upload supported image formats and receive an emotion prediction result.

Does EMORA store my images permanently?

EMORA is designed with privacy in mind. You can adapt backend handling according to your storage policy.

Who is EMORA for?

EMORA is intended as a supportive tool for neurodivergent users, caregivers, educators, and anyone exploring assistive emotion-recognition solutions.

Is EMORA a replacement for human communication?

No. EMORA is meant to assist understanding, not replace empathy, conversation, or professional guidance.

Can the system improve over time?

Yes. With ethical feedback design and further refinement, the model and user experience can continue improving.
