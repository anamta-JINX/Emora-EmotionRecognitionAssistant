Meet the Team

EMORA was built with the goal of creating a calm, supportive, and intelligent tool for emotion recognition. This page introduces the people and the purpose behind the project.

Anamta

Co-creator of EMORA, contributing to the project’s vision, design direction, and platform development with a focus on accessibility and meaningful impact.

Eman

Co-creator of EMORA, helping shape the system’s structure, user experience, and project execution from concept to implementation.

Our Purpose

EMORA exists to support neurodivergent individuals by reducing uncertainty in emotional interpretation and providing real-time clarity through technology.
