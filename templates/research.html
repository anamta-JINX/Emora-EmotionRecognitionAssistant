Research & Development

EMORA is rooted in the idea that emotion recognition technology can assist communication when designed ethically, clearly, and with user sensitivity in mind.

Problem Statement

Many individuals, especially some neurodivergent users, may face difficulty interpreting facial expressions consistently in everyday social interactions. EMORA aims to provide calm, immediate support through real-time AI analysis.

Technical Approach

The system combines face detection and convolutional neural network-based classification to identify emotional states from facial input. The workflow is designed for both live and uploaded image interaction.

Training Methodology

EMORA uses multiple datasets, structured training-validation-testing stages, and refinement through feedback-oriented data design to improve model stability and generalization.

Future Scope

Future improvements may include better contextual emotion understanding, expanded expression support, accessibility refinements, and broader testing across diverse real-world settings.
